## What it is
ISO 13485 is the internationally recognized standard for quality management systems in the design and manufacture of medical devices. It outlines requirements that help organizations ensure their medical devices meet customer and regulatory demands for safety and efficacy (iso.org/standard/59752).

## Who it applies to
"Any organization involved in the design, production, installation, and servicing of medical devices and related services", and also suppliers and external parties that provide product, "including quality management system-related services" to such organizations (FAQ, iso.org/standard/59752). Compared with ISO 9001, it is tailored to medical-device regulatory and safety requirements and "emphasizes meeting regulatory as well as customer requirements, risk management, and effective process validation" (FAQ).

Third-party certification by certification bodies; ISO does not certify. Whether a national regulator accepts or requires ISO 13485 certification (e.g. via MDSAP or EU MDR/IVDR) is set by those regulators, not by ISO, and is not verified here (UNVERIFIED).
