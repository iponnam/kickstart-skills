## What it is
ISO/IEC 27018 "provides guidance for protecting personally identifiable information (PII) in public cloud services, specifically when the cloud service provider acts as a PII processor. Built on ISO/IEC 27002, this standard outlines controls and principles tailored to cloud environments" (iso.org/standard/27018). The 2019 abstract ties these controls to "the privacy principles in ISO/IEC 29100".

## Who it applies to
"Public cloud service providers acting as PII processors, as well as organizations evaluating cloud providers or seeking to ensure their own compliance when outsourcing data processing" (FAQ). The 2019 abstract adds that it applies to all types and sizes of organizations providing processing services as PII processors via cloud computing "under contract to other organizations", and that PII controllers may have additional obligations it does not cover.

## Assessment and certification
Guidance document; ISO's pages describe it as complementing an ISO/IEC 27001-based ISMS and do not describe stand-alone certification. Any certification/attestation against 27018 is scheme-specific (UNVERIFIED).
