## What it is
Kenya's data protection statute, administered by the ODPC. The only official text that could be read was the ODPC home page: "Organisations processing personal data must register with the ODPC."

## Who it applies to
UNVERIFIED (Act s.4 application not readable). The ODPC home page confirms organisations processing personal data must register.
