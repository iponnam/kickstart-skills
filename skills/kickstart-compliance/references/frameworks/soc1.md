## What it is
"SOC 1 is an examination of controls at a service organization that are likely to be relevant to user entities' internal control over financial reporting. SOC 1 reports are specifically intended to meet the needs of entities that use service organizations (user entities) and the CPAs that audit the user entities' financial statements (user auditors)" (SOC 1 topic page). It is performed under the AICPA attestation standards in SSAE No. 18, which "recodifies and supersedes SSAE Nos. 10 – 17" with listed exceptions (SSAE 18 download page). The SOC 1 examination section is AT-C section 320 (section number from AICPA search listing; full text behind login).

## Who it applies to
Service organizations whose services and controls are "likely to be relevant to user entities' internal control over financial reporting" (topic page), e.g. payroll, payments, claims processing, hosted financial systems. The SOC 1 Guide covers subservice organizations ("inclusive method or the carve-out method") and "software-as-a-service providers" (SOC 1 Guide page).

## Engineering obligations
SOC 1 has no fixed control catalogue: the service organization defines control objectives and controls, and the auditor tests them. The rows below come from the SOC 1 Guide and management information pages; detailed requirements are in AT-C 320 (behind login).

| ID | Obligation | Official reference |
|---|---|---|
| SOC1-01 | Prepare a description of the service organization's system, including all relevant elements | "Information for Service Organization Management in a SOC 1® Engagement" page ("determining whether the description of the service organization's system includes all...") |
| SOC1-02 | Define control objectives that are reasonable for the services provided | SOC 1 Guide page ("reasonableness of control objectives") |
| SOC1-03 | Describe key system outputs (reports/data user entities rely on); omission leads to a report paragraph | SOC 1 Guide page ("when a description of key system outputs is omitted") |

## Assessment and certification
Performed by a licensed CPA firm (service auditor) as an examination engagement; output is a SOC 1 Type 1 or Type 2 report (SOC 1 Guide page refers to "a type 1 or type 2 report") for use by user entities and their user auditors. Type 1 = fairness of the description and suitability of design as of a date; Type 2 adds operating effectiveness over a period: UNVERIFIED (AT-C 320 definitions behind login). Cadence is not set by AICPA on free pages; commonly annual, aligned to customers' fiscal years: UNVERIFIED.
