## What it is
Seychelles' modern data protection law, replacing the never-commenced Cap 57 Act (s.55). It sets principles, data subject rights including portability, privacy by design, records and logs, DPIA, security, breach notification, DPO, cross-border transfer limits and administrative fines. The Information Commission "is the competent authority to enforce and implement this Act" (s.5(1)).

## Who it applies to
s.3: "the processing of data through automatic or semi-automatic means, and to the processing of data through non-automatic means within Seychelles, which data forms part of a filing system whether managed by a private or public data controller." s.4 excludes criminal investigations, national security and purely personal activity.

## Deadlines and numbers
| What | Value | Official reference |
|---|---|---|
| Breach notification to regulator | "not later than 72 hours after having become aware of it" | s.43(1) |
| Notification to individuals | "promptly inform the data subject" (breach likely to affect a significant number of individuals) | s.44(1) |
| System restoration after interruption | "in any case not exceeding 72 hours" | s.42(4)(b) |
| Child age | "below the age of 18 years" | s.23(1) |
| Transition period | "within a period of 18 months from the coming into operation of this Act" | s.56(1) |
| Non-compliance with Commission order | administrative fine "not exceeding SCR200,000" | s.7(7) |
| General offence | "imprisonment not exceeding 2years or to a fine of level 5 on the Standard Scale" | s.50(1) |

## Assessment and certification
No certification. The Commission enforces, issues orders and administrative fines (s.51), and consults on high-risk processing.
