## What it is
The second edition of the ISO/IEC information security management system requirements standard. ISO's life-cycle record lists "ISO/IEC 27001:2013 — Withdrawn", "ISO/IEC 27001:2013/Cor 1:2014 — Withdrawn" and "ISO/IEC 27001:2013/Cor 2:2015" under "Previously", with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 under "Now — Published" (iso.org/standard/27001).

## Who it applies to
Historically, any organization operating an ISMS. As a withdrawn edition it should not be used for new implementations or certifications; map existing work to ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

## Engineering obligations
No separate obligations are kept for the withdrawn edition. Use iso-27001-2022. Mapping of 2013 Annex A controls to 2022 controls is in the paywalled ISO/IEC 27002:2022 text and is not verified here.
