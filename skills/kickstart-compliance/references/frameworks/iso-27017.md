## What it is
ISO/IEC 27017 "provides guidance for implementing information security controls in cloud services. It builds on ISO/IEC 27002 by adding cloud-specific guidance and additional controls for both cloud service customers and cloud service providers" (iso.org/standard/27017). It clarifies how controls apply across customer and provider environments where responsibilities, infrastructure and operations are divided between parties.

## Who it applies to
Cloud service customers and cloud service providers. "ISO/IEC 27017 applies to all cloud deployment models, including public, private and hybrid cloud environments"; in private cloud "some controls may need to be adapted to reflect the relationships and responsibilities between internal departments" (FAQ). Controls "should be selected and applied according to the organization's risk assessment and any relevant legal, regulatory, contractual or cloud-specific security requirements".

## Assessment and certification
ISO/IEC 27017 is a guidance (controls) document; ISO's pages do not describe certification to it. In practice it is assessed as an extension within an ISO/IEC 27001 certification audit, but that scheme detail is not stated on ISO's pages (UNVERIFIED).
