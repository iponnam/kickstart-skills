## What it is
ISO 9001 is a globally recognized standard for quality management; its requirements define how to establish, implement, maintain and continually improve a quality management system (QMS) (iso.org/standard/9001). The sixth edition, ISO 9001:2026, was published on 16 September 2026 and, per ISO, focuses on clarity, emphasizes quality culture and leadership, "separates risk and opportunities", and improves alignment with other ISO management system standards.

## Who it applies to
"Any organization seeking to improve its quality management, meet customer and applicable statutory and regulatory requirements, and enhance customer satisfaction"; suitable for all sizes and sectors; also used by certification bodies and other third parties to assess conformity (FAQ, iso.org/standard/9001). Sector adaptations named by ISO include ISO 13485 (medical devices) and ISO/IEC/IEEE 90003 (computer software).

## Assessment and certification
Certification is optional: "organizations implementing ISO 9001 can choose whether or not to seek certification" (FAQ). Certificates are issued by accredited conformity assessment bodies; ISO does not certify. Audit guidance is published by the ISO 9001 Auditing Practices Group (ISO/TC 176 and the International Accreditation Forum). The IAF transition period for ISO 9001:2026 (if any) is set by IAF/certification bodies; iaf.nu is not reachable from this sandbox, so it is UNVERIFIED.
