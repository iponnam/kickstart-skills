## What it is
Québec's private-sector privacy statute, substantially modernised by Law 25 (2021, c. 25). It adds a person in charge of personal information protection, governance policies, privacy impact assessments, confidentiality incident notification and register, privacy by default, transparency on automated decisions and tracking technologies, portability, and large administrative penalties and fines. The CAI supervises and enforces.

## Who it applies to
Any "person carrying on an enterprise" holding personal information about others (s.1, s.3.1). The confidentiality incident regulation applies to public bodies and to "any person carrying on an enterprise" under P-39.1 (Reg. s.1).

## Deadlines and numbers
| What | Value | Official reference |
|---|---|---|
| Notification to CAI | "must promptly notify the Commission d'accès à l'information" (risk of serious injury) | s.3.5 |
| Notification to individuals | must "notify any person whose personal information is concerned by the incident" | s.3.5 |
| Access request response | "promptly and not later than 30 days after the date the request is received"; silence is deemed refusal | s.32 |
| Incident register retention | "kept for at least 5 years after the date or time period when the body became aware of the incident" | Reg. s.8 |
| Minor consent age | "under 14 years of age" | s.4.1 |
| Administrative penalty max | "$50,000" natural person; otherwise "$10,000,000 or, if greater, ... 2% of worldwide turnover" | s.90.12 |
| Penal fines | "$5,000 to $100,000" natural person; otherwise "$15,000 to $25,000,000, or, if greater, ... 4% of worldwide turnover" | s.91 |
| Phased dates | CAI: "en vigueur depuis septembre 2022" (first set); "Depuis septembre 2023" (PIA for transfers outside Québec, consent, anonymisation, governance); "Depuis le 22 septembre 2024" (portability) | CAI page |

## Assessment and certification
No certification. CAI inspects, investigates, imposes monetary administrative penalties (s.90.1 ff.) and institutes penal proceedings (s.92).

## Notes
- Checklist line "PIPEDA / Law 25: ... privacy impact assessment before transfers outside Quebec; breach register kept; consent ... separately for each purpose" is right for Law 25 but understates the PIA scope: Law 25 also requires a PIA for any information system or electronic service delivery project involving personal information (s.3.3). Register retention (5 years) and portability (from 22 Sept 2024) are missing.
- The exact 22 September 2022 / 2023 day-level dates for each group of provisions are in the transitional provision of S.Q. 2021, c. 25 (s.177), not re-fetched; the CAI page gives months (Sept 2022, Sept 2023) and 22 September 2024 for portability.
