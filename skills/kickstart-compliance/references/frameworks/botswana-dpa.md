## What it is
Botswana's data protection legislation. The National Assembly Order Paper of 8 August 2024 describes the Data Protection Bill, 2024 (Bill No. 19 of 2024) as "An Act to make provision for the continuation of the Information and Data Protection Commission; to regulate the protection of personal data and to ensure that the privacy of individuals in relation to their personal data is maintained; and to provide for all matters incidental thereto" ("Published on 26th July, 2024"; Minister for State President; Second Reading, resumed debate).
