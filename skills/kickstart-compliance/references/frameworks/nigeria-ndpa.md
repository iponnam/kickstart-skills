## What it is
Nigeria's data protection statute, establishing the NDPC. The GAID 2025, issued by the NDPC under the Act, sets detailed compliance measures (registration tiers, compliance audit returns, DPO, DPIA, breach notification, cookie notices, training).

## Who it applies to
s.2(2): where the controller or processor "is domiciled in, resident in, or operating in Nigeria"; processing "occurs within Nigeria"; or the controller/processor "is not domiciled in, resident in, or operating in Nigeria, but is processing personal data of a data subject in Nigeria". s.3(1) excludes purely personal or household processing. GAID Art. 8(2): "Operating in Nigeria" includes a controller or processor "who targets a data subject in Nigeria". Data controllers/processors of major importance (DCPMI) are designated by the NDPC: the Guidance Notice designates those with a filing system that process "more than Two-Hundred (200) data subjects in six (6) months", offer certain ICT services, or operate in listed sectors; tiers UHL, EHL, OHL.

| What | Value | Official reference |

## Assessment and certification
Self-run compliance audits; UHL/EHL file CAR with NDPC (CAR may be filed through licensed Data Protection Compliance Organisations per GAID, not re-checked); NDPC may issue a Compliance Audit Returns Certificate (GAID Art. 10(13)). NDPC investigates and orders penalties (s.46-48).
