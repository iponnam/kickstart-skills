## What it is
ISO/IEC 20000-1 specifies requirements for an organization to establish, implement, maintain and continually improve a service management system (SMS). The requirements cover the planning, design, transition, delivery and improvement of services to meet the service requirements and deliver value (abstract, iso.org/standard/70636).

## Who it applies to
Per the official abstract, it can be used by: customers seeking assurance on service quality; customers wanting a consistent service-lifecycle approach from all their service providers "including those in a supply chain"; organizations demonstrating capability to plan, design, transition, deliver and improve services; organizations monitoring, measuring and reviewing their SMS; conformity assessment bodies; and training/advice providers. "Organization" means the organization in the scope of the SMS that manages and delivers services to customers; it can be part of a larger organization (e.g. a department), and is also known as a service provider.

## Assessment and certification
Third-party certification by a certification body against ISO/IEC 20000-1; the abstract lists "an organization or other party performing conformity assessments against the requirements specified in this document" as an intended user. ISO itself does not certify. Certification cycle and surveillance cadence are set by certification/accreditation rules, not on the ISO catalogue page (UNVERIFIED here).
