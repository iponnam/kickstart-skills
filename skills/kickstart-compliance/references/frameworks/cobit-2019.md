## What it is
ISACA describes COBIT as "the premier framework for the governance and management of enterprise IT" and states that "Effective governance over information and technology is critical to business success" (COBIT Resources page). "COBIT 2019 is specifically designed to play well with others": guidance is given to integrate other standards, guidelines and regulations into a COBIT-based governance solution (same page). The COBIT Core Model has "40 governance/management objectives. Each governance/management objective and its purpose are defined and then matched up with the related process, Alignment Goals and Enterprise Goals" (description of *COBIT 2019 Framework: Governance and Management Objectives*).

## Who it applies to
Voluntary, any enterprise. Tailoring uses "design factors" (Implementation Guide description: "the new terminology and concepts of COBIT 2019, including the design factors"), described in the *COBIT 2019 Design Guide: Designing an Information and Technology Governance Solution*. ISACA also publishes Focus Area guides relevant to engineering teams: "COBIT Focus Area: DevOps", "COBIT Focus Area: Information Security", "COBIT Focus Area: Information & Technology Risk", and a small/medium enterprise focus area (COBIT Resources page).

## Assessment and certification
No organisational certification by ISACA for COBIT. ISACA offers individual credentials: "COBIT Foundation Certificate" and "COBIT Design & Implementation Certificate Program" (COBIT Resources page). Capability levels for processes (COBIT performance management, CMMI-based) can be self-assessed or assessed by auditors: detail UNVERIFIED.
