## What it is
ISO/IEC 42001 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) within organizations; ISO calls it "the world's first AI management system standard" (iso.org/standard/42001). It is a management system standard using the Plan-Do-Check-Act methodology and, rather than covering specific AI applications, provides a way to manage AI-related risks and opportunities across an organization (FAQ).

## Who it applies to
"Organizations of any size involved in developing, providing, or using AI-based products or services. It is applicable across all industries and relevant for public sector agencies as well as companies or non-profits" (FAQ). Asked whether it applies to all AI systems: "Yes, it's designed to be applicable across various AI applications and contexts" (FAQ).
