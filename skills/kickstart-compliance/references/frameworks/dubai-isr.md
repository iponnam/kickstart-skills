## What it is
DESC describes the ISR as providing "all Dubai Government Entities with the standards to ensure continuity of critical business processes, and minimize information security related risks". It is "a technology neutral framework", "presents the minimum requirements for information security controls", and "is broken down into thirteen domains" spanning three classes: Governance, Operation and Assurance. It was "formalized pursuant to Resolution No. 13 of 2012", and under "Dubai Law No. 11 of 2014" DESC maintains and updates it.

## Who it applies to
Per DESC: "applicable to all Dubai Government Entities, including but not limited to employees, consultants, contractors and visitors who are not government employees but are engaged with it through various means", and "to any government information regardless of its type and medium". Private suppliers are covered through that engagement. Cloud providers serving Dubai government and semi-government entities must comply with the separate DESC CSP Security Standard, which is based in part on "ISR:2017 v.02".

## Engineering obligations
The ISR text is only available from DESC by "EMAIL REQUEST", so control-level obligations could not be checked. Only the programme-level duties stated on the DESC page are listed.
| ID | Obligation | Official reference |
|---|---|---|
| ISR-01 | Run an applicability review of ISR domains and controls to decide which apply | DESC ISR page |
| ISR-02 | Base control selection on risk assessment results ("right-fit" implementation; control cost lower than the risk or information value) | DESC ISR page |
| ISR-03 | Implement Governance domains (structuring and managing information security) | DESC ISR page |
| ISR-04 | Implement Operation domains (technical and non-technical controls chosen by risk assessment) | DESC ISR page |
| ISR-05 | Implement Assurance domains (checking that implemented controls work as intended) | DESC ISR page |
| ISR-06 | Cover all government information in any medium and all business functions | DESC ISR page |
| ISR-07 | Cloud services for Dubai government: comply with, and may certify against, the DESC CSP Security Standard (ISO/IEC 27001-style certification scheme) | DESC Certifications page |
| ISR-08 | Related mandatory DESC standards where in scope: IoT Security Standard (Dubai government and semi-government), ICS Standard (operators of critical infrastructure/OT) | DESC Standards & Policies page |

## Assessment and certification
Not stated for the ISR on the DESC page (UNVERIFIED). DESC runs a CSP Security Standard certification for cloud providers, following the ISO/IEC 27001 accreditation/certification scheme.
