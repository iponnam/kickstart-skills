## What it is
ISA says the ISA/IEC 62443 series "define requirements and processes for implementing and maintaining electronically secure industrial automation and control systems (IACS)", set best practices and "provide a way to assess the level of security performance" (isa.org). The standards are developed by the ISA99 committee and IEC TC 65 WG 10 and adopted by IEC; "In 2021, IEC recognized the series as a horizontal standard" (isa.org). A founding principle is "shared responsibility" across asset owners, product suppliers, integrators and service suppliers (isa.org).

## Who it applies to
Voluntary. Parts map to roles (isa.org titles; IEC webstore abstracts):
- Asset owners: Part 2-1 "Security program requirements for IACS asset owners" (IEC 62443-2-1:2024; ANSI/ISA-62443-2-1-2024).
- Service providers / integrators: Part 2-4 "Security program requirements for IACS service providers" (IEC 62443-2-4:2015+AMD1:2017).
- System design: Part 3-2 risk assessment, zones and conduits; Part 3-3 system security requirements and security levels.
- Product suppliers: Part 4-1 "Secure product development lifecycle requirements"; Part 4-2 "Technical security requirements for IACS components".
- Also: 1-1 terminology/concepts/models (ISA-62443-1-1-2007 / IEC TS 62443-1-1), TR 2-2 protection scheme (ISA-TR62443-2-2-2025), TR 2-3 patch management.

## Assessment and certification
No single certifying body. Product (4-2), development process (4-1), system (3-3) and service-provider/asset-owner programs can be certified by third-party schemes; the IEC webstore lists IECEE Test Report Forms (e.g. "IECEE TRF 62443-2-1A:2024", "IECEE TRF 62443-4-1B:2023") and ISA operates ISASecure (named on isa.org navigation).
