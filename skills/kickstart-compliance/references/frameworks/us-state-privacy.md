## What it is
A list of US states with comprehensive consumer privacy laws in force or enacted. California and New Jersey were checked in the first pass. In the follow-up pass (2026-11-06) 14 more states were checked on their legislature hosts; Connecticut (www.cga.ct.gov returned "upstream request failed"), Montana and New Hampshire (both redirect to hosts the proxy refuses) and Texas (statutes.capitol.texas.gov serves a JavaScript app that never loaded the statute) remain UNVERIFIED. No third-party source was used for facts.

## Who it applies to
Thresholds differ by state (usually counts of consumers whose data is processed, sometimes with a revenue-from-sale condition). See each statute. CA: § 1798.140(d)(1). NJ: C.56:8-166.5.

## Assessment and certification
No certification under any listed law. Enforcement by state Attorneys General (California also by the CPPA). Several statutes have cure periods that expire on their own dates (UNVERIFIED except NJ: 30 days to cure until the first day of the 18th month after the effective date, §14(b); and Delaware: during the period from the effective date to "December 31, 2025" the Department of Justice issues a notice of violation where a cure is possible, 84 Del. Laws c. 197 § 12D-111(b)).
