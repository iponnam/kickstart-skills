## What it is
SP 800-171 "provides federal agencies with recommended security requirements for protecting the confidentiality of CUI when the information is resident in nonfederal systems and organizations" (CSRC abstract). The requirements are "a subset of the controls that are necessary to protect the confidentiality of CUI" derived by tailoring the SP 800-53 moderate baseline (Sec. 2). Rev. 3 introduces organization-defined parameters (ODPs) and aligns requirement numbering with 800-53 families.

## Who it applies to
"The requirements apply to components of nonfederal systems that process, store, or transmit CUI or that provide protection for such components. The security requirements are intended for use by federal agencies in contractual vehicles or other agreements established between those agencies and nonfederal organizations" (CSRC abstract). It binds a company only through a contract or agreement (e.g., DFARS 252.204-7012 for DoD contractors).

## Assessment and certification
SP 800-171 has no certification of its own. Assessment procedures are in SP 800-171A (Rev. 3 for Rev. 3). For DoD, assessments occur under CMMC (32 CFR Part 170): Level 2 self-assessment or C3PAO certification against Rev. 2, plus DoD Assessment Methodology scores in SPRS under DFARS 252.204-7019/7020.

## Notes
