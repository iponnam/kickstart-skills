## What it is
ISO/IEC 27001 is "the world's best-known standard for information security management systems (ISMS). It defines requirements an ISMS must meet" (iso.org/standard/27001). An ISMS conforming to it "preserves the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information by applying a risk management process and gives confidence to interested parties that risks are adequately managed" (FAQ). The official abbreviation is ISO/IEC 27001 (jointly published by ISO and IEC under JTC 1/SC 27).

## Who it applies to
"Companies of any size and from all sectors of activity" (iso.org/standard/27001). ISO notes it enables organizations to "establish an information security management system and apply a risk management process that is adapted to their size and needs" (FAQ "Who needs ISO/IEC 27001?"). It covers information "in all forms, including paper-based, cloud-based and digital data" (FAQ benefits).

## Assessment and certification
Certification is voluntary: "companies implementing ISO/IEC 27001 can decide whether they want to go through a certification process" (FAQ). Certificates come from conformity assessment bodies; one from an accredited body adds confidence "as an accreditation body has provided independent confirmation of the certification body's competence" (FAQ). ISO does not issue certificates. Audit cycle (stage 1/stage 2, surveillance, 3-year recertification) is set by certification/accreditation rules, not on ISO's page (UNVERIFIED).
