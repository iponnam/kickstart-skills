## What it is
SP 800-53 Rev. 5 "provides a catalog of security and privacy controls for information systems and organizations to protect organizational operations and assets, individuals, other organizations, and the Nation from a diverse set of threats and risks" (CSRC abstract). The controls "can be implemented within any organization or system that processes, stores, or transmits information" (Sec. 1.1). Control baselines (low, moderate, high, plus a privacy baseline) are published separately in SP 800-53B.

## Who it applies to
"The use of these controls is mandatory for federal information systems in accordance with Office of Management and Budget (OMB) Circular A-130 ... and the provisions of the Federal Information Security Modernization Act [FISMA]" (Sec. 1.1). A federal information system is "an information system used or operated by an agency, a contractor of an agency, or another organization on behalf of an agency" (Sec. 1.1, fn. 10). National security systems are not subject to FISMA here (fn. 11). Others "are encouraged to consider using these guidelines" (fn. 12). It is the control basis for FedRAMP baselines.

## Assessment and certification
Federal systems are assessed and authorized under the NIST Risk Management Framework (SP 800-37) using assessment procedures in SP 800-53A, resulting in an Authorization to Operate by an agency official. There is no NIST certification for private organizations. Cloud services for federal agencies are authorized through FedRAMP using 800-53-based baselines.
